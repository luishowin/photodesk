# Spec review — 2026-09-05

A read-through of `ARCHITECTURE.md` at v0.3, before any code existed. Kept because `DECISIONS.md` records *what* changed and this records *why* — and because several findings are still open.

Line references are to v0.3 and no longer resolve against the current file.

---

## Contradictions (all five fixed in v0.4)

**1. §5 described two execution models.** Line 180 made per-layer application *stage 13*, after global stages 2–12. Line 184 said a global adjustment is a layer with `mask: null` sitting first, one code path. Those are different pipelines, in the section marked as the frozen contract.

**2. Spike A's gate did not test what §0 freezes.** Criterion 2 required the edit-state struct be swappable "without editing shader code", but stage *order* is encoded in the shader chain and `engine/` is read-only (`:579`). All three criteria could pass while "pipeline order is explicit and versioned" became unimplementable. Fixed by a fourth criterion.

**3. Two shader sources.** `src-tauri/src/engine/` vendored upstream's WGSL chain (it is in the §2.1 classification list, `:71`) while `shaders/photodesk/` was called the source of truth (`:582`). Both cannot render.

**4. §12.2 failed by construction.** Sharpen and noise reduction at halo 32 are resolution-dependent; grain rendered full-res then downsampled averages to nothing while grain at proxy stays visible. The test would have gone red immediately and been muted — making `:546` ("without it the invariant is a comment") self-fulfilling.

**5. Mask evaluation point undefined.** `luminance` and `color` are the only components computed from pixels, and nothing said which pixels. Source, layer input and final image give three different masks.

## Gaps

**HDR gain maps** — fixed by decision, not by implementation. The stated native subject is an iPhone HEIF (`:44`) and the thesis is to take the manufacturer's rendering as the starting point (`:136`). Modern HEICs carry an ISO gain map. Ignoring it silently means the app opens a photo looking flatter than the Photos.app the user just left, and they conclude the colour pipeline is broken. v1 discards it deliberately and says so.

**Front-end framework** — never named anywhere, in a document titled "master spec for the coding agent", while §10 and §11 specify interaction down to `Shift`-drag being 0.1× travel. Now §16 #8, resolved by Spike A.

**AI mask cache key** (`:403`) — `feather` sat in `input_state_hash`, so dragging the feather slider would have invalidated the embedding and re-run SAM. And the key had no resolution component, so a proxy-resolution mask could silently serve a full-resolution export. Both fixed.

**60 fps budget** (`:326`) — "four or five passes" is per *layer*, not per frame. Now bounded at 6 layers.

**History persistence** — `:252` made history append-only deltas; the sidecar example had no history field. Now session-only.

**"No banding"** (`:98`) — listed under "Tests, all automated" with no numeric definition. Now three assertions on the output ramp.

**Spike C's success criterion** (`:106`) — frame rate only, no requirement to verify RGBA16F render targets. Spike B could have frozen f16 against a preview path unable to render to it.

**Golden corpus** (`:538`) — 96 reference images with no stated resolution and no lfs decision. Both fixed before the first `--bless`, which is the only cheap time.

**`Provider::execute` was synchronous** (`:355`) while §11 forbids blocking the canvas. Resolved by policy rather than by making the trait async.

**Licensing sequencing** (`:639`) — the AGPL review was deferred to "before redistribution", but the fork decision is the point of no return. Moved to gate Spike A.

## Still open

- Estimates. The roadmap sums to ~22–23 weeks. The 2-week Phase 0 budget is the most optimistic line in the document: Spike A alone is fourteen subsystems plus a headless test binary, and Spike C now needs a WebGL2 harness before it can measure anything. Three weeks is the honest number.
- §10.1 permits clipping warnings in colour because "colour is the data", but clipping overlays are drawn *on* the photograph — the most adjacent possible position, and the exact objection §10.1 raises against a red mask overlay. The reasoning that produced the white-at-35% mask overlay applies here too and has not been applied.
- `providers/` sits at the repository root while `src-tauri/src/ai/` holds the registry and routing. Whether `providers/` is a Cargo workspace member is unstated.
